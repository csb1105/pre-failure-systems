# Visualizations

This directory contains conceptual diagrams, research illustrations, and operational models developed for the Pre-Failure Systems research project.

The figures are intended to be viewed sequentially.

---

## Figure 1. Repository Overview

The introductory visualization presenting the core concepts of Pre-Failure Systems. It illustrates how instability develops before visible failure and introduces the major components explored throughout the repository.

---

## Figure 2. Drift vs. Correction Capacity

Illustrates the relationship between accumulating operational drift and declining correction capacity. The figure demonstrates why failure emerges when correction can no longer compensate for increasing system deviation.

---

## Figure 3. The Intervention Window

Demonstrates how opportunities for successful intervention contract as instability accumulates. The figure emphasizes that intervention effectiveness depends primarily on timing rather than intervention intensity.

---

## Figure 4. The Recovery Landscape

Illustrates how recovery potential changes as systems move through regions of increasing instability. Recovery depends on remaining within operationally reachable regions before irreversible boundaries are crossed.

---

## Figure 5. The State Space of Pre-Failure

Represents organizational behavior as movement through an operational state space. The visualization highlights trajectories, recovery regions, constraint boundaries, and the preservation of maneuver under changing system conditions.

---

## Figure 6. The Pre-Failure Early Warning Framework

Integrates signal detection, operational monitoring, intervention timing, and decision support into a unified early warning architecture designed to identify instability before visible failure.

---

## Figure 7. The Integrated Pre-Failure Systems Framework

The complete operational architecture showing how all six preceding models connect into a unified framework for detecting instability, preserving maneuver, and enabling effective intervention before irreversible failure.
